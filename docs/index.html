The Problem

Our memories deserve better than a camera roll

📷

Photos without context

Albums record time and location but leave no space to capture the experience, the mood, or why a place mattered to you.

🌫️

No sense of exploration

There is no visual record of how much of the world you have actually explored, making travel feel unmemorable over time.

🔗

Sharing is disconnected

Recommending a place today means sending a link, a screenshot, or a text — all divorced from your personal story about it.

⏱️

Moments pass too fast

By the time you open a notes app, the feeling is gone. There is no quick way to capture a memory before the moment disappears.

🏆

No shared sense of adventure

Existing journalling apps are isolated — there is no way to see how your exploration compares to friends or feel a sense of shared adventure.

How It Solves It

Every feature, purposefully designed

My Journey turns each problem into a delightful interaction — powered by GPS, sensors, live APIs, and a connected community.

📍 Check-In System

Capture the moment, in full

Long-press anywhere on the map to create a check-in. Add an emoji label, a place name, personal notes, and photos from your gallery or camera. The app asks if you want to record the live weather — so "it was raining in Tokyo" is captured forever, tied to that exact GPS coordinate.

12 emoji labels Photo gallery Real-time weather Personal notes
🌫️ Exploration Path

Watch your world unfold

The map starts covered in a soft fog. As you physically move through the world, the fog clears to reveal where you have been — a growing, visual memoir of your entire life's movement. Built with a custom Flutter painter and BlendMode rendering, persisted across sessions.

Fog-of-war overlay GPS trail Persisted locally
📳 Shake to Check-In

Too good to pause for your phone?

Shake your device and My Journey instantly saves a check-in at your GPS location — no tapping, no typing. The accelerometer sensor detects a genuine shake (above 2.7g, three spikes in 800ms) and saves the moment with weather attached before you've even put your phone back in your pocket.

Accelerometer sensor Auto weather capture 10s cooldown
🌍 World & My Race

How far have you really gone?

The World screen tallies every city and country your feet have touched, complete with flag emojis and drill-down lists. The My Race leaderboard compares your cities and countries count against friends in real time — with a gold, silver, bronze podium for the top three explorers.

Cities & countries Friends leaderboard Podium ranking Firestore real-time
📢 Community Noticeboard

Your story becomes someone else's discovery

Share any check-in to a public noticeboard — a live community feed where anyone using My Journey can read your notes, discover the place, and import it to their own collection. Or share a branded card image directly to WhatsApp, Instagram, or any iOS share sheet.

Technical Stack

Built on a connected foundation

My Journey integrates multiple external services to create a seamless bridge between the physical and digital worlds.

🗺️

Google Maps SDK

Real-time GPS, custom markers, satellite and dark map styles

🌤️

OpenWeatherMap API

Live weather fetched at check-in time and displayed on map

📍

Google Geocoding API

Reverse geocoding of GPS coordinates to city and country names

🔐

Firebase Auth

Google Sign-In with session persistence and full account deletion

☁️

Cloud Firestore

Real-time sync for user profiles, friends, and the noticeboard feed

💾

SQLite (sqflite)

On-device storage for check-ins, scoped per user UID

📳

Accelerometer Sensor

sensors_plus package — detects shake gestures at 2.7g threshold

📲

Flutter / Dart

Cross-platform framework, iOS primary target, Android APK available
